# Workflow: Social Media Content — compact

Handles LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest, and Threads. Load `SKILL.md` first.

## Platform and fold

Put one complete value proposition before the visible fold; never use a cliffhanger.

| Platform | Hard limit | Fold / hook target |
|---|---:|---:|
| LinkedIn | 3,000 | 130–140 chars |
| Instagram | 2,200 | 100–115 of ~125 chars |
| TikTok | 4,000 | 80–90 of ~90 chars |
| Facebook | 63,206 | 400–450 of ~477 chars |
| YouTube | 5,000 | 130–150 of ~157 chars |
| X/Twitter | 280 | 260–270; no fold |
| Threads | 500 | 450–480; no fold |
| Pinterest | 500 description | 40–50 of ~50 chars |

## Formats

- **LinkedIn:** data-backed hook, 3–5 short paragraphs or parallel EAV bullets, one discussion question, and 3–5 niche hashtags.
- **X:** one complete post or a 3–5 post thread. Each post stands alone; use distinct data/steps and one final CTA. Avoid hashtags in promoted posts.
- **Instagram/TikTok:** visual hook, scannable tips/context, one CTA, and 3–5 relevant hashtags. Instagram uses no more than five.
- **Facebook:** complete hook, optional longer context, direct CTA; omit hashtags unless the campaign needs one.
- **YouTube:** keyword-led hook, chapters/timestamps for long video, relevant links, and CTA.
- **Pinterest:** keyword-led title/description and a “save” or click action.

## Guardrails

Use SVO, exact verified facts, clear entity/attribute/value statements, short paragraphs, and one primary action. Remove modal verbs, vague hype, conversational filler, and invented statistics. Use no more than 1–2 purposeful emojis per section. Keep hashtags to 3–5 and make them relevant.

## QA

Check complete hook before fold, platform hard limit, one CTA, no hype/modality/filler, no invented facts, correct hashtag/emoji count, and standalone thread posts. Use this hashtag mix when needed: broad category, niche entity, specific feature, brand/campaign, and a genuinely relevant trend.
