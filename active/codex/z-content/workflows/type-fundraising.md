# Workflow: Fundraising Appeal

Handles: donation pages, charity campaigns, cause-driven email, cause-driven ad copy, fundraising appeals.

Load `SKILL.md` first. Load `reference/conversion-copy-rules.md` for the full conversion layer. This file provides fundraising-specific structure and evidence rules.

---

## Structural Model

```
1. Title: [Concrete gift or change] — [deeper meaning]
2. Opening Human Moment (stakes, dignity, imagined scene signaled)
3. Why This Matters (daily and emotional consequence)
4. What Support Makes Possible (concrete mechanism)
5. First Donation Ask (conviction line + CTA)
6. Meaning of the Gift (item to human consequence)
7. Visible Chain of Work (numbered steps: preparation, delivery, access)
8. Proof and Trust (organization, record, images, fund-use note)
9. Deeper Meaning (faith, dignity, community, continuing benefit)
10. Final Close (human choice + final ask + CTA)
11. Additional Details (concise FAQ, fund-use, sharing option)
```

## Title Rule

Title format: **[Concrete gift or change] — [deeper meaning or intended community].**

- Wrong: "Help Us Fund Our Project"
- Right: "Give Clean Water — A Sadaqah Jariyah for Displaced Families"

## Opening Human Moment

Help the donor picture a truthful daily moment. Signal illustrative scenes with "Imagine" or "For a family...". These are scenarios, not case evidence.

## First Donation Ask

Surface after the first complete reason to act. Include a conviction line plus CTA. Place after "What Support Makes Possible".

## Visible Chain of Work

Present preparation, delivery, access, or service as numbered steps. Connect donation to action without inventing a guaranteed individual outcome. This is the strongest conversion element from the WFA water-appeal lessons.

## Proof and Trust

Name the organization, relevant record, verified images, and current facts. Keep transparency concise and late in the argument. Excessive caveats on the emotional path weaken the appeal.

## Deeper Meaning

Use faith, dignity, remembrance, community, or continuing benefit responsibly. Do not promise divine reward, acceptance, or a guaranteed spiritual result.

## Final Close

Restate the human choice and final ask. Place all additional details (FAQ, fund-use note, sharing option) after this close.

## Fundraising QA Additions

- [ ] **Title format:** Concrete gift + deeper meaning.
- [ ] **Opening moment:** Signal-imagined scene, not invented case evidence.
- [ ] **First ask:** CTA after the first complete reason to act.
- [ ] **Visible chain:** Numbered steps connecting donation to action.
- [ ] **FAQ placement:** After the principal close, not before.
- [ ] **Dignity:** No humiliating imagery, savior language, or guilt threats.
