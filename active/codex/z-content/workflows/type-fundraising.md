# Workflow: Fundraising Appeal — compact

Handles donation pages, charity campaigns, cause-driven email, and cause-driven ads. Load `SKILL.md` and `reference/conversion-copy-rules.md`.

## Argument

```text
concrete gift/change → signaled human stake → need/consequence
→ mechanism → first ask → visible work chain → proof/trust
→ dignity/meaning → final ask → details
```

- Title: `[Concrete gift or change] — [deeper meaning or community]`.
- Signal illustrative scenes with `Imagine` or `For a family`; never turn them into case evidence.
- Name what support supplies, installs, delivers, funds, or maintains.
- Place the first donation ask after the first complete reason to act.
- Show preparation, delivery, access, or service as numbered steps.
- Name organization, record, verified images, current facts, and fund-use note.
- Place FAQ, fund-use, and sharing details after the principal close.

## Safety and QA

Keep one donor action and one destination. Use verified impact claims only. Preserve dignity and agency; avoid humiliation, savior language, guilt threats, fabricated stories, false urgency, guaranteed outcomes, and guaranteed spiritual rewards. Use faith or remembrance language sincerely and verify quotations.

Pass only when title, opening scene, first CTA, visible work chain, proof, FAQ placement, and ethical emotional language are complete.
