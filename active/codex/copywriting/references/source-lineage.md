## Adaptation Rules

- Use concepts, not copied branded language or complete frameworks.
- Keep organization-specific evidence authoritative.
- Reference pages provide structural inspiration, not facts.
- Select narrative depth versus brevity by awareness and decision complexity.
- Revalidate source URLs and licenses before public skill distribution.
