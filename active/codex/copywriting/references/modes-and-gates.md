# Modes and Gates

## Mode Router

### PLAN

Use for strategy, outlines, content architecture, campaign concepts, or a
durable plan before writing.

Deliver:

1. objective and one primary conversion;
2. audience and awareness state;
3. source and claim ledger;
4. one big idea and promise;
5. emotional and rational argument;
6. section and image sequence;
7. CTA cadence;
8. open decisions and publish blockers.

Do not write full copy unless the user also asks for it.

### CRAFT

Use for net-new copy. Complete the pre-plan, evidence, and pre-craft gates,
then write the whole reader journey. Offer alternative headlines or CTAs only
when they expose a real strategic choice.

### REWRITE

Inventory what must be preserved: meaning, facts, voice, legal language,
links, assets, and successful sections. Mark each block `KEEP`, `TIGHTEN`,
`MOVE`, `REPLACE`, or `DROP`. Return a complete page.

### AUDIT

Inspect the rendered page and source when available. Rank findings:

- `P0`: misleading, unsupported, broken, or materially harmful;
- `P1`: weak promise, unclear audience, buried CTA, or broken narrative;
- `P2`: generic proof, poor order, weak headings, or image mismatch;
- `P3`: rhythm, polish, or minor consistency.

Show a concrete replacement for every high-impact finding.

### BUILD

Create the smallest implementation the target platform can reproduce. Bind
editable blocks, formatting limits, image paths, links, and responsive
behavior. Validate structure, assets, links, desktop, and mobile. Keep local
preview proof separate from publication proof.

### FUNDRAISING

Use `emotional-conversion.md` and the fundraising template. Apply dignity,
consent, and claim discipline strictly:

`human moment -> urgent need -> tangible response -> visible mechanism ->
meaning of gift -> repeated action -> additional details`

## Gate Outcomes

- `PASS`: enough evidence and clarity to continue.
- `PASS_WITH_ASSUMPTIONS`: assumptions are low-risk and recorded.
- `DEPENDENT_BLOCK`: one claim or section cannot proceed; other work can.
- `STOP`: missing authority, rights, or a decision would materially change the
  result.

A gate is a decision point, not a mandatory new document.

## Source Ladder

Prefer:

1. current organization records and approved campaign facts;
2. current live page or authenticated source;
3. supplied official documents and approved prior copy;
4. primary public sources;
5. dated saved snapshots;
6. competitor/reference pages for structure only.

Competitor copy is inspiration, not evidence and not text to imitate.

## Awareness and Length

- High awareness: state the offer, proof, and CTA quickly.
- Problem aware: deepen the consequence, then explain the mechanism.
- Low awareness: use a vivid human or market situation before the offer.
- High trust: shorten institutional explanation.
- Low trust: add mechanism, proof, and real objection handling.

Long copy is useful when each section adds belief, proof, emotion, or clarity.
